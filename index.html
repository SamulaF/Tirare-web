The Problem

Over 3 million Indian laborers still rely on manually pulled carts. They burn thousands of calories daily, endure extreme spinal strain, and often earn under ₹300.

  • Average lifespan: 52 years
  • Back injuries: Affect 78%
  • Air pollution exposure: 4× WHO safe limit

India Is Heating Up

With temperatures crossing 47 °C in parts of India, street labourers pulling metal carts face rising risk of heatstroke and collapse.

“It’s not just hot. It’s inhuman.” What does dignity mean when survival itself is at risk?

Animals Deserve Dignity Too

Across many cities, horses, donkeys and oxen are still used to pull overloaded carts in extreme heat — often without water, rest, or care. When they collapse, they’re beaten instead of rescued.

This is not tradition. It’s abuse — normalized on our streets.

Tirare stands for all lives. Our electric transition is also an animal‑welfare revolution.

Our Solution

Electric Cargo Trikes — Built for Real India

From brand‑new electric trikes to retrofitted carts, our modular system restores dignity, saves lives and boosts income.

  • Zero down‑payment rent‑to‑own options
  • Battery swap + lifetime service model
  • Works for new builds and upgrades
