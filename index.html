Retire the Exploitative Cart.

Tirare replaces human- and animal-pulled carts with modular electric cargo trikes — restoring health, income, and dignity to India’s informal workforce.

Our Solution Impact Data

The Problem

Over 3 million Indian workers still pull carts manually — burning extreme calories, suffering spinal injuries, and earning less than ₹300/day.

  • 78% report chronic back pain
  • Average working lifespan: ~52 years
  • Heat exposure far beyond WHO limits

Animals Deserve Dignity Too

Horses, oxen, and donkeys are still forced to pull overloaded carts in extreme heat — collapsing without care.

This is not tradition. It is normalized cruelty.

Our Solution

Modular electric cargo trikes — designed for India’s streets, informal economy, and dignity.

  • Rent-to-own, zero upfront cost
  • Battery swap & service included
  • Retrofit compatible with existing carts
